# CargaExpress Supply Chain

CargaExpress is a third-party logistics (3PL) visibility and operations project designed to consolidate shipment information from multiple carriers, normalize carrier-specific events, and support operational decisions across brands and distribution centers.

## Business context

The operating context described in the project backlog includes **60 brands, 9 distribution centers, and 12 carriers**. Each carrier can report different status names, codes, timestamp formats, and event sequences. The Control Tower must turn those differences into a consistent view of shipment progress.

## Project goals

- Provide a consistent, traceable view of shipment status across carriers.
- Normalize external carrier events into a canonical status catalog.
- Apply controlled status-transition rules and detect anomalous events.
- Support SLA calculation, delivery-risk identification, and responsibility analysis.
- Integrate warehouse workflows for orders, shipping labels, picking, fulfillment, and dispatch.
- Prepare reliable data for operational reporting and periodic billing or settlement processes.
