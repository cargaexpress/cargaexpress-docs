# Business Context and Scope

## Operating context

| Dimension | Project context |
|---|---|
| Business model | Third-party logistics (3PL) |
| Brands | 60 |
| Distribution centers | 9 |
| Carriers | 12 |
| Primary challenge | Carrier events are heterogeneous and must be normalized |
| Operational outcomes | Shipment visibility, SLA monitoring, risk, responsibility analysis, and settlement support |

These figures come from the project context shared during backlog discovery. They are planning context, not a claim that all integrations are already connected.

## Business problem

Shipment events arrive from carriers using different vocabularies, codes, date formats, and event ordering. Without a canonical model, a team cannot reliably compare shipment progress, identify delayed orders, explain exceptions, or calculate business rules consistently.

## Product scope

### Control Tower

- Ingest carrier data through push, pull, and file-based integration patterns.
- Preserve the source event and normalize it to a canonical shipment status.
- Apply transition rules and flag out-of-order or otherwise anomalous events.
- Calculate SLA and risk indicators when the relevant rules and data are available.
- Keep traceability from an external event to the normalized event and affected shipment.

### Warehouse Management System (WMS)

The backlog includes warehouse workflows for orders, shipping labels, picking, fulfillment, and dispatch. One order may be associated with multiple shipping labels; the data model must not assume a one-to-one order-to-label relationship.

### Reporting and financial processes

The business context includes responsibility analysis, penalties, and periodic billing/settlement. These are business outcomes, but their delivery must follow the approved sprint scope. In particular, penalties and a Power BI dimensional model were explicitly outside the known Sprint 3 scope.

## Out of scope or not confirmed

- Kubernetes as the primary deployment objective.
- A confirmed numeric cloud budget: no approved cost estimate was available in the retrieved backlog context.
- Any claim that end-to-end ingestion is complete. The latest project status shared in conversation said ingestion had not yet been implemented.
- Assigning team roles: the team had not defined roles at the time this documentation context was shared.
