## Cross-cutting rules confirmed in project discussions

- Keep the carrier catalog and brand catalog consistent; prevent invalid or orphaned references.
- Support natural business keys and global identifiers where needed to correlate entities across systems.
- Seed data must use a fixed seed and be reproducible and idempotent.
- Carrier events may arrive out of order and may use different date formats.
- Carrier-specific state catalogs must be mapped to the canonical catalog.
