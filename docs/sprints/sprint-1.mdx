# Sprint 1 — Discovery and Design

## Objective

Establish a shared understanding of the business problem, document the initial requirements, agree on canonical shipment-state behavior, and create the technical foundation for implementation.

## Captured work areas

- Document the business context, actors, and operational needs.
- Define and review business rules `RN-01` through `RN-10`.
- Define and review functional requirements `F1` through `F7` and quality requirements.
- Establish the canonical shipment-state catalog and transition rules.
- Draft the conceptual data model and clarify natural keys versus global identifiers.
- Define carrier integration patterns: push, pull, and file-based ingestion.
- Produce architecture v1 and at least two ADRs.
- Establish repository conventions and the team's pull-request workflow.
- Create a fixed-seed, reproducible, idempotent seed script, including representative data defects where required for testing.

## Proposed story range

The discovery/design discussion proposed `US-01` through `US-15`. The captured topics include business/actors, requirements, quality, model, integration, architecture/ADRs, repository setup, and seed data. The accessible context does not preserve the exact ID-to-topic mapping, so the approved backlog must remain authoritative.

## Definition of Done captured in the project context

- Documentation is reviewed and traceable to requirements.
- Architecture v1 is documented.
- At least two ADRs are written and reviewed.
- The conceptual data model is documented.
- The seed script runs reproducibly and idempotently.
- User stories have clear Given/When/Then acceptance criteria.

## Acceptance evidence to attach

- Approved backlog and traceability matrix.
- Canonical-state catalog and transition matrix.
- ERD or conceptual data model.
- Accepted ADRs.
- Repeatable seed execution evidence.
- Repository and PR-template evidence, if required by the approved Sprint 1 backlog.
