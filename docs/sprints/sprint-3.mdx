# Sprint 3 — Orchestration, SLA, and Cloud Delivery

The captured Sprint 3 backlog covers `HU-26` through `HU-40` and was associated with weeks 4–5 of the six-week project timeline.

## Story groups

| Story IDs | Focus captured in the backlog summary |
|---|---|
| `HU-26`–`HU-30` | Orchestration and asynchronous processing using Airflow, Celery, and RabbitMQ. |
| `HU-31`–`HU-33` | Business-day SLA rules and at-risk order logic. |
| `HU-34`–`HU-40` | AWS optimization and deployment, including Docker, ECR, RDS, ECS/Fargate, and CI/CD. |

The original backlog contains acceptance criteria, dependencies, MoSCoW priorities, and technical tasks. Those exact per-story details must be copied from the approved backlog; they are not reconstructed here where the wording was not preserved in the accessible summary.

## Explicit exclusions from this Sprint 3 scope

- Penalty implementation.
- A Power BI dimensional model.
- Kubernetes as the primary project goal.

These exclusions apply to the captured Sprint 3 scope. They do not imply that penalties or reporting are irrelevant to the wider business vision.

## Validation focus

- Scheduled or asynchronous work is observable and recoverable.
- Business-day calculations use the agreed calendar and SLA definitions.
- At-risk order decisions are traceable to the rule and source data that produced them.
- Cloud deployment and CI/CD configuration are reproducible and do not expose secrets.
- Infrastructure costs are reviewed against the team's approved budget before deployment.
